Welcome to My Website

Hi! My name is Neel Chandwani, and I am a junior at Northeastern University in Boston, Massachusetts. I am studying Computer Science, and I am especially interested in Software Engineering and Machine Learning. I built this website to share a little bit about who I am, what I study, and what I do outside of the classroom.

Northeastern has been a great place for me to grow. Between classes, co-op preparation, and campus life, every semester keeps me busy, but I always try to make time for the things that help me relax. Boston is a fun city to be a student in, with a lot of places to explore and really good food to try.

Outside of school, my favorite hobby is swimming. I have been swimming for 14 years, and it has become one of the most important parts of my life. Getting in the pool helps me clear my head after a long day of classes and keeps me healthy and focused. Swimming has also taught me discipline, patience, and how to keep pushing when things get hard.

On the Swimming page, you can read more about why I love swimming, the strokes I enjoy most, and a quick look at how much I swim each week. Thanks for stopping by, and feel free to reach out using the contact links below!
